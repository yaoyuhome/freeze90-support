Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy describes how the Freeze90 mobile app handles information. Freeze90 is a self-guided wellness app and does not require an account.

Information stored on your device

Freeze90 stores session history and app preferences locally on your device. A history record may include the session date and duration, breathing technique, selected scene tag, a self-rated calmness score, and an automatically generated completion note. Preferences may include breathing settings, haptics, and Live Activity settings.

You can delete individual history records or clear all history in the app. Uninstalling Freeze90 removes its local app data from the device. If iCloud Backup is enabled, Apple may include app data in a device backup according to your iOS settings and Apple's policies.

Rage Shredder text

Text entered in Rage Shredder is held temporarily while that screen is open. Freeze90 does not save that text in session history or transmit it to the developer. The text is cleared after shredding or when you leave the screen.

Information sent off your device

The reviewed version of Freeze90 does not send session history, settings, or Rage Shredder text to developer-operated servers. The app does not include advertising or analytics SDKs.

Live Activities use Apple's iOS system to display the current session on supported devices. Freeze90 does not send Live Activity information to a developer server.

Children

Freeze90 is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not require an account and does not transmit the local history described above to the developer.

Changes to this policy

If the app's information practices change, this policy will be updated. The effective date above indicates when it was last revised.
