Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Freeze90 does not require an account.

Where is my session history stored?

Session history and preferences are stored on your device. You can delete individual records or clear your history in the app.

Does Freeze90 save what I type in Rage Shredder?

Freeze90 does not save or transmit the text you type there. The text is cleared after shredding or when you leave the screen.

Is Freeze90 medical or emergency care?

No. Freeze90 is a self-guided wellness tool. It does not diagnose or treat a medical or psychological condition and is not an emergency service. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
